Karachi

Karachi is Pakistan's largest city and economic powerhouse. Stretching along the Arabian Sea, this vibrant metropolis generates the largest share of the nation's GDP. Known as the "City of Lights," it features a bustling nightlife, energetic culture, and rich historical roots as the country’s original capital.
